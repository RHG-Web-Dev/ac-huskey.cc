Twitter bot that provided real-time updates on school closures and holiday breaks in the Eastern Tennessee area. The bot was built using Node.js and the Twitter API, and it utilized web scraping techniques to gather information from local schools enlisting keywords about being closed.
{/* card */}

<br />
School closures were then tweeted out to followers, providing a valuable service
to the community during inclement weather and other emergencies.
<br />
The project was a significant learning experience, teaching me how to work with
APIs, manage data, and create engaging user experiences through a social media
platform.
<br />
The service is now archived as twitter's apis are no longer free to use, but it
was a fun and rewarding project that helped me grow as a developer and provided
a useful service to the community during its active years.
